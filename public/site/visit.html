The last hundred feet

Look for the lighted case.

Eighty four booths sit under one roof, so the address only gets you to the door. Inside, look for the lighted counter case and the number on the sign above it: Booth 18

If Bill is in, he is behind that case. Wednesdays and Sundays, 10 to 6.

Ask, and anything comes out. In a mall where most cases stay locked, that is the point: Bill wants you to pick a piece up, turn it over and read the tag he wrote for it.

Booth 18 · Paradise Antique Mall

The weekly three

Before you leave the booth on Wednesday or Sunday. Takes about five minutes.

  1. Pick three pieces

    Anything new in the case, or anything you enjoyed finding. It does not have to be the expensive stuff. The odd ones are usually the ones people ask about.

  2. Take two photos of each

    One of the whole piece. One up close on your tag, so the writing is easy to read. Six photos in total.

  3. Text them to A.J.

    All six in one message. That is the whole job. He does the rest, and you never have to open a computer.

What makes a photo work

  • No flash. The case light or the daylight is better, and the flash blows out the glass.
  • Get closer than feels right. Fill the whole screen with the piece.
  • Take two of each and send both. One is always sharper.
  • Tag right side up and in focus. If you can read it on the phone, so can we.
  • Set it on the counter rather than shooting through the glass, if it is easy to reach.

If you feel like it, talk for thirty seconds

Hold the phone up and just say it out loud, no script, then send that too. This is the part people actually want.

  1. What is it, and roughly what years is it from?
  2. Where did you come across it?
  3. What should somebody know before they buy one?

Once in a while, not every week

  • A photo of the booth itself when it looks good after you have tidied it.
  • Anything that sold, and what it went for. People like knowing.
  • Anything you turned down and why. That is a good story every time.

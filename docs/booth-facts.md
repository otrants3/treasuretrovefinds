# What the Picker Road episode teaches about the booth

Source: docs/picker-road-transcript.txt (their segment runs 14:17 to 29:50).
Note: the segment is the two hosts narrating. Bill is present but barely on mic,
so this is not a source for his voice. It is a source for facts and third party praise.

## The thesis, confirmed by strangers

The hosts noticed, unprompted, exactly what the site is built around:

- Bill puts research cards in the cases: bottle dating guides ("1860 to 1880,
  1880 to 1890, 1900 and above") with magnifying glasses tied on so you can
  check the bottles yourself.
- "Explanations for everything" throughout the booth.
- Quotable, attributed praise (use at most once on the site):
  "This is a vendor who really knows his stuff." (Picker Road, YouTube)
- They bought five things: the Benson's Queen Mary candy tin ($30), a Steiff
  wiggy owl, cat eye marbles, a 1930s-40s brass mailbox, an amethyst vase.

## A real policy worth putting on the site

"He said that all the cases were open, so we could open anything up that we
want." The cases are open. Ask. That is a differentiator in a mall where
everything is locked.

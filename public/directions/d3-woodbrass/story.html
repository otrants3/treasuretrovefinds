Decades of Homework

Bill has been collecting for decades. For most of them it was his alone.

Then the kids grew up and moved out, and the weekends went quiet. Bill and Lisa started filling them together: estate sales, garage sales, anywhere old things turn up. It became how they reconnected.

Stop by the house any night and you will find Bill at the kitchen table, a tag in one hand and a reference book in the other. Lisa is a room away, working her side of it.

For years they talked about opening a shop someday. It stayed talk. In 2025 the talk finally happened: Booth 18, moved from Long Island to Las Vegas. The New York plates still hang on the back wall.

The homework shows up on the tags. Maker, year, what he found, and the price, in Bill's handwriting.

The bottle shelves carry dating guides with magnifying glasses tied on, so you can check for yourself.

"I like to branch out because I learn the history of something when I learn about it. Because I can study it and then I'm pretty good at it."

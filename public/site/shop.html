What is actually listed

Strangers have bought from them 183 times.

What they have not done is build a big online catalogue. There are three pieces listed on Etsy today. That is not a soft launch or a teaser, it is simply how far Bill has gotten with the camera.

Pieces go up as he photographs them and writes down what he found out about each one. Meanwhile the booth in Las Vegas holds far more than the shop has ever listed: 1920s to 1950s toys, trucks, cars, trains, collector bottles, advertising, and Christmas and Halloween from the 1920s to the 1960s.

So if you are looking for a catalogue to browse, this is not that yet. If you are looking for a specific thing, keep reading.

Call Bill and describe it.

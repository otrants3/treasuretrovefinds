Treasure Trove Finds · Redesign

Three directions, all built from the real booth

Same content in all three: Bill's own words about what he collects, the real pieces with the tags he wrote for them, what is moving in the market, where the booth is, and how to reach him. What changes is the point of view. Open each one, then tell me which one feels like your parents.

Direction 01

The Litho

Built like the box the toys came in. Fat 1930s advertising type, four colour litho printing, the booth's real reds and yellows turned up loud.

The risk: the headings print slightly out of register, the way real toy boxes did.

Loud · Toy forward · Hard to ignore
Direction 02

The Keeper

A magazine profile of your dad. Big photography, long captions, his handwriting reproduced on the tags, the whole thing filed like his own records.

The risk: every photo carries a typed accession line, like a museum that never got formal.

Quiet · Bill forward · Built to be trusted
Direction 03

Under Glass

The website is the case. Dark walnut and brass, shelves that stay dim until you move along them, and the light comes up on whatever you are looking at.

The risk: the whole page is dark, and the shelf lighting is the thing people will remember.

Dark · Collector forward · The most unusual

What they carry

Dig through it.

Both booths are packed floor to ceiling. Tap anything here and you get what Bill knows about it.

Toys and trains, bottles and glass, advertising and service station pieces, vintage jewelry, dolls and plush, kitchen and housewares, tools and hardware, furniture, paper and ephemera. Most of it sits between $10 and $100.

Why this booth is different

Every piece leaves with its record.

Most vendors write a price on a tag. Bill writes down what he found out: the maker, the year, how the thing works, what one sold for at auction.

He has loved history since he was a kid on Long Island, and he still does the homework on every piece before it goes in the case.

The cases are open. Ask for anything and it comes out. In a mall where most cases stay locked, that is the whole point: he wants you to look closely.
